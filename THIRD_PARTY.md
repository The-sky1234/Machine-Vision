# Third-party data and model notice

## NEU-DET

The trained detector was evaluated on the NEU surface defect database
published by Northeastern University (China). The publisher provides the
dataset for download and asks users to cite the associated work, but does not
state a software/data license. This repository therefore does **not**
redistribute any NEU-DET image or annotation. Obtain the dataset from the
[official page](https://faculty.neu.edu.cn/songkc/en/zdylm/263265/) for
research use and contact the publisher for broader rights.

## Ultralytics YOLO

`models/neudet_yolo11n.pt` was fine-tuned from Ultralytics YOLO11n pretrained
weights with the `ultralytics` Python package. Ultralytics distributes its
open-source package under AGPL-3.0 and also offers enterprise licensing.
Closed-source commercial use requires a compatible licensing decision.
